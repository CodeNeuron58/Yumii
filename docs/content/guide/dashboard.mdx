The island is for *talking*; the **dashboard** is for everything else. Open
it from the sliders button on the island (hover the top edge), or from the
tray icon → **Dashboard**. Every control saves on its own — there's no
"save all" that could rewrite settings you didn't touch.

## Models

Her pipeline, left to right — **01 Hear → 02 Think → 03 Speak** — each
stage marked *on device* or *cloud*:

* **Hear** — the speech-to-text engine (local Whisper with its size, Groq
  Whisper, Vosk, NVIDIA Nemotron Speech, Moonshine).
* **Think** — the provider and model. With a key, the list is live from the
  provider; **Free only** filters to free models. Switching applies from her
  next reply, no restart.
* **Speak** — the voice engine (Kokoro on your machine, ElevenLabs, CAMB.ai).

Below the pipeline: her **Voice** (the seven Kokoro voices) and her
**Personality** (applies live — or just ask her to switch).

<Callout type="info" title="Live vs. restart">
Personality, the model you think with, and the island settings apply live.
Changing the ears, the voice engine, the approval mode or a key needs a
restart — the dashboard shows a banner when one is due (quit from the tray,
reopen).
</Callout>

## Behaviour

* **The island** — how long answers stay up after she finishes (2–8 s, or
  until you close them), and whether her reply text shows under the strip.
* **Asking first** — which tools need your OK: *outside actions* (default:
  web search, email, apps), *everything*, or *never*.
* **Shortcuts** — the hotkey and the spoken commands (stop, repeat, new chat,
  mute).

## Tools

Web search (Tavily when you've added a key, DuckDuckGo otherwise) and apps
through [Composio](https://composio.dev): connect Gmail, Calendar, Notion,
GitHub, Slack — or any toolkit by name — with a browser sign-in, and disable
them again. The full walkthrough lives in [Tools & Permissions](/guide/tools).

## Chats

Every conversation, newest first, the current one marked **Now**:

* **View** — the full transcript. Tool use appears as small events between
  the spoken turns.
* **Resume** — switches the live conversation to that session.
* **Rename / Delete** — housekeeping. Delete asks once, then removes the
  conversation for good.

## Memory

Everything she has learned about you, each fact with its category
(identity, preference, habit, relationship, goal). **Edit** a fact she got
slightly wrong, or **Forget** it. This tab is the transparency guarantee:
there is no hidden profile.

## Keys

Every key Yumii can use, grouped by mind, voice and tools — Nebius, Groq,
Tavily, Composio and the rest. Fields are **write-only**: you see a masked
`gsk_···6K5Q` for what's stored, and saving a new value replaces it. Keys
live in `~/.yumii/auth.json`, readable only by your user.

## Privacy

A plain account, built from your actual settings, of what stays on your
computer and what leaves it — and to whom.

## Browser access

The dashboard is served by the local backend, so it also works in a plain
browser at `http://127.0.0.1:8000/dashboard.html` while Yumii is running.
Add `#chats` (or any tab name) to open straight to a tab. It's bound to
localhost and protected by an origin allowlist; other devices and websites
can't reach it.
