You've [installed Yumii](/get-started/installation). The installer already
asked you to pick her mind, ears and voice — the rest sets itself up.

<StepsList>
  <Step icon="download" title="She downloads her voice">
    On first launch the island at the top of your screen shows a progress bar
    while her local models download — the voice (~330&nbsp;MB), and the ears
    too if you picked local Whisper. One-time; after this they run offline
    on your CPU.
  </Step>
  <Step icon="star" title="One key wakes her up">
    If you pasted a key in the installer, skip this. Otherwise a card
    appears: <em>"I just need a mind to think with."</em> Click
    <strong> Get your Nebius Token Factory key</strong> (opens in your
    browser), paste it, and hit <strong>Wake her up</strong>. (Prefer another
    provider? "more options" opens the dashboard.)
  </Step>
  <Step icon="face-smile" title="Say hello">
    Allow microphone access and talk. She listens continuously — no wake
    word, no push-to-talk. Try interrupting her mid-sentence: she stops and
    listens. That's not a bug, that's the point.
  </Step>
</StepsList>

## Things to try in your first five minutes

* **"Switch to tsundere."** — her whole personality changes on the spot.
* **"Remember, my sister's name is Riya."** — then later: "what's my
  sister's name?" She saves it on your machine; you'll see it in the
  dashboard's Memory tab.
* **Tell her something that's coming up.** "I've got an interview Friday."
  Talk to her after — she knows what happened last time.
* **"What's the weather in Tokyo today?"** — a permission prompt appears
  before she searches. That gate guards every external action she takes.
* **"Do my conversations leave my computer?"** — she tells you the truth
  about where her mind runs.
* **Press `Ctrl+Shift+Space`** — the hotkey hides and summons her from
  anywhere.

## Where everything lives

| Surface | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **The bar** | The conversation — at the top of your screen; talk, interrupt freely |
| **The dashboard** | Tray → Dashboard (or the sliders button on the island): models, behaviour, tools, chats, memory, keys, privacy |
| **The tray icon** | Show/hide, dashboard, quit |

<Callout type="info" title="Credits and free tiers have limits">
New Nebius Token Factory accounts start with trial credit; Ollama and Groq
have free tiers with weekly or daily quotas. If she hits a limit she says so
with a clear error card — switch providers in the dashboard or top up. See
[Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting).
</Callout>
