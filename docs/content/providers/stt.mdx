Two separate things happen when you speak, and only one of them is
configurable:

1. **Speech detection (VAD)** — noticing that you're talking and when you
   stopped. This is *always* local: a tiny Silero neural network runs on your
   CPU for every audio frame. It ships inside the app; no setup, no network,
   ever.
2. **Transcription (STT)** — turning your finished utterance into text. This
   is the pluggable part.

Pick the transcriber in **Dashboard → Models → Hear**.

## The backends

| Backend | Privacy | Speed | Accuracy | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Local Whisper** *(default)* | ✅ Fully local | ~1–2s per utterance | Good | Nothing — downloads on first launch, with a progress bar |
| **Groq Whisper** | ☁️ Cloud | ~0.3s per utterance | Best (whisper-large-v3) | A Groq key |
| **Vosk** | ✅ Fully local | Instant, streams as you speak | Weakest — not recommended | Nothing — model downloads on first use |
| **NVIDIA Nemotron Speech** *(experimental)* | ✅ Fully local | True streaming, live text as you speak | Strong (English; a multilingual variant exists) | A ~470&nbsp;MB model download |
| **Moonshine** *(experimental)* | ✅ Fully local | Fast on weak hardware | Good (English) | A ~110&nbsp;MB model download |

The two experimental ears run through sherpa-onnx on your CPU. They load and
transcribe correctly, but haven't had the real-microphone testing the others
have — pick them in the dashboard if you'd like to try.

### Local Whisper

The private default: `faster-whisper` running on your CPU. Choose the model
size in settings — `tiny` (fastest), `base` (the balanced default), or
`small` (most accurate, slower). The 1–2 seconds it takes to transcribe is
the biggest chunk of her response delay on this backend. On a fast machine
she also shows your words live while you speak; on a laptop CPU she
measures the cost once at startup and skips the live text rather than
slowing your turn down.

### Groq Whisper

Dramatically better ears from one free key: whisper-large-v3-turbo in the
cloud, roughly five to ten times faster than local and noticeably more
accurate. It also filters non-speech well — humming and background noise get
discarded instead of hallucinated into words. If you're comfortable with
audio going to Groq, this is the best experience.

### Vosk

Fully offline *and* streaming — your words appear on screen as you say them.
The trade-off is real: recognition quality is clearly below Whisper. It
exists for the niche where offline + live partial text matters more than
accuracy.

## Tuning your environment

* **Echo:** the app applies echo cancellation, so speakers are fine — but
  headphones make barge-in crisper since she never hears herself.
* **Background noise:** an energy gate plus the VAD ignores fans and hums.
  Distinct voices (TV, roommates) can still trigger listening — that's
  physics, not configuration.
* **Getting cut off mid-thought?** She closes your turn after a short
  silence. Keep the sentence flowing, or just keep talking — interrupting
  her reply is always allowed.
